# BestBefore

**A privacy-focused, AI-powered digital memory ecosystem.**

BestBefore lets people archive, curate, and experience personal media — photos, videos, and reflections — across extended timelines. Instead of a static, chronological feed, memories become something you can lock, organize, search by meaning, and revisit on your own terms.

## ✨ What We Build

- **⏳ Time Capsules (Future Lock)** — Lock photos, videos, and written notes until a future date. Content stays inaccessible until it unlocks, turning ordinary uploads into scheduled retrospective moments.
- **🗝️ Concept Rooms (Konsept Odalar)** — Thematic vaults instead of static feeds. Share access with specific people or collaborator groups via unique digital keys.
- **🔍 AI Semantic Search** — Find memories by description, visual concept, or mood — not just filenames and manual tags.
- **🎨 Artist Portfolios** — A dedicated presentation layer for creative work, giving artists a structured way to showcase visual media with lasting context.
- **🔒 Secure Storage** — Media is persisted via cloud storage (AWS S3 / Firebase), with metadata, access permissions, and auth handled by a cross-platform backend.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

`Node.js` · `MongoDB` · `Kotlin` · `Swift`
